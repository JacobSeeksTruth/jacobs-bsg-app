About This App

I call this collection the Lost Books of Scripture. Driven by my curious nature, I’ve always wondered if there is more to the story beyond simply reading the Bible.

Interestingly, some of these so-called “lost” books actually appear in older versions of the Ethiopian Bible—raising the question of why they are absent from most modern English Bibles. Either way, I want to learn more about these stories, as they add valuable depth and context to the history of our beginnings.

I gathered and compiled this app to organize all of this information in one central place—primarily for my own benefit. Most of the material comes from publicly available archives that anyone can access for free. I simply organized it and packaged it into a clean, easy-to-use app. I’m happy to share it with all of you at no cost. I believe it can help anyone in their Bible studies by providing additional puzzle pieces that enrich the overall story with greater context.

Happy reading, listening, and studying.
