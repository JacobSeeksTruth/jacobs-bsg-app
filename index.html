About This App

Hi, I'm Jacob. I created this App to help me study and learn God’s Word, and now I want to share it with you for free.

Join me on this journey as we explore these Bible study guides together and grow closer to God through Scripture.

Volume Selection:
Each volume is a study guide with various verses to study and memorize and includes a devotional to read after completed the curriculum.

Free Download:
Every study guide volume includes a free, downloadable PDF accessible via the download button.

These PDFs not only contain the scripture verses featured in the app, but also include extra information, exclusive study content, and a special message from me that isn't available anywhere else.

Happy studying, and may God bless your journey!
