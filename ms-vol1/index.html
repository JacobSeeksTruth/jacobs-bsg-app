Walking in the Love of God Through Faithful Obedience

Congratulations on finishing every verse! These verses form a beautiful path of faith.

Devotional

In the beginning God created all things (Genesis 1:1). This same God so loved the world that He gave His only Son so that whoever believes in Him will have eternal life (John 3:16).

We have all sinned and fall short of God’s glory (Romans 3:23). The wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus (Romans 6:23).

Trust in the Lord with all your heart (Proverbs 3:5). Seek first His kingdom and righteousness (Matthew 6:33). The Lord is your Shepherd; you shall not want (Psalm 23:1).

You can do all things through Christ who strengthens you (Philippians 4:13). And we know that for those who love God, all things work together for good (Romans 8:28).

Receive His love. Follow Jesus in trusting obedience. He will lead, strengthen, and guide you every step of the way.
